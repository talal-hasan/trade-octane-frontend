How a re-route works

  1. Pick the approver whose work is stuck Everything they hold shows at once — every table and level, not one at a time.
  2. Choose who takes it Only active people who share their role are offered, as on the old screen. Each shows how much they already hold.
  3. Check the rows, then re-route Each queue moves only if it still holds exactly the rows counted — otherwise it is left untouched and you are asked to look again. Every move is audited.
